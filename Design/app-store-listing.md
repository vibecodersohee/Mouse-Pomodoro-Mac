## Description
Move Your Mouse is a gentle pomodoro timer that lives in your menu bar, with a pixel-art mouse who keeps you company while you work.

Focus for a while, take a real break, and watch your little friend celebrate with you. Every session earns cheese, and cheese unlocks cozy new backgrounds. Nothing is ever taken away. There are no broken streaks, no guilt, only progress.

PLAN YOUR DAY
• Choose how many sessions you want today, from 1 to 20
• Set your own focus (1–90 min) and break (1–30 min) lengths
• Sessions chain automatically: focus, break, focus, until your plan is done
• Wrap up early whenever you like and still get credit for the effort

A COMPANION, NOT A TASKMASTER
• Your mouse works beside you during focus and sleeps when you pause
• During breaks, tap to make it hop, feed it a snack, or move your cursor to make it dance
• Name your mouse the first time you start
• A little daily note of encouragement

EARN CHEESE, UNLOCK BACKGROUNDS
• +1 cheese for every finished session, plus a bonus for keeping a daily streak
• Even ending a session early earns half a cheese
• Your office slowly grows as you complete more sessions
• Seasonal parks change with the time of year
• Spend cheese in the shop on rooftops, cafeterias, studios, and Easter, Halloween and Christmas scenes
• Milestone celebrations at 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1000 sessions

SEE YOUR WEEK
• A weekly heatmap of your sessions, focused time, and cheese
• Your level and total sessions at a glance
• Save a daily summary image to share or keep

BUILT FOR THE MENU BAR
• See the time left right next to the menu bar icon
• Shrink to a tiny floating strip that stays on top while you work
• Notifications when focus or break ends, even when the app is in the background

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics
• Everything stays on your Mac

Requires macOS 14 or later.

## App Review notes
This is a menu bar app with no Dock icon. After launching, click the small mouse icon at the right side of the menu bar to open it. No sign-in or account is needed. On first use the app asks for a name for the mouse, then tap "Plan your sessions" to begin.
